# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straight with people and assume they are being straight with you. Disagree about the work, not about the person. Take the most reasonable reading of what someone wrote, especially when they are writing in their second language.

Not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, sexual attention, publishing someone's private details, and sustained disruption of a thread.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, the pull requests, the discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Email conduct@cortad.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private.

We will confirm we received it within two working days and tell you what we decide. Depending on what happened that is a private word, an edit or deletion of the content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban.

If a report concerns a maintainer, it goes to the same address and that maintainer takes no part in deciding it.
